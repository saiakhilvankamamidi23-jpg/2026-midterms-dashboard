What the numbers mean

Called: A winner has been projected by the election-results provider. These count toward secured seats.

Leading: A candidate is ahead in votes but the race has not been called. Leads can change.

Unavailable: A source may not yet publish a race, or a third-party connection may be down. Missing is never counted as zero.
